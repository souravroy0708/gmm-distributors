Nirmala Agri Vita Green Gold: Humic Granules Guide for Paddy Farmers

Nirmala Agri Vita Green Gold is one of the humic-granule products in the GMM Distributors portfolio. This guide focuses on the information available from the product label and how farmers can use that information responsibly. It's not a fertilizer in the traditional NPK sense — it's a granulated soil conditioner designed to help the plant use nutrients more efficiently and build a stronger root system from the start.

This guide covers what's actually in the pack, why it's become popular across Hooghly's paddy belt, and exactly when to apply it for the best result.

What Is Nirmala Agri Vita Green Gold?

Nirmala Agri Vita Green Gold is a granulated soil conditioner that combines five active components in one pack:

  • Humic acid — improves soil structure and helps roots access nutrients already present in the soil.
  • Fulvic acid — a smaller, more mobile organic acid that supports nutrient transport within the plant.
  • Amino acids — building blocks that support plant metabolism and stress recovery.
  • Seaweed extract — a natural source of trace elements and growth-supporting compounds.
  • Bentonite — a clay mineral that helps bind and slow-release the blend in the soil.

Together, these are marketed for vegetables and fruit crops as well as paddy — but it's in paddy fields across Arambagh and neighbouring blocks that the product has built its strongest following.

"It's not about replacing your fertilizer bag — it's about helping the plant actually use what you're already applying."

Why It Works Well for Paddy

Paddy is a heavy feeder with a relatively short window to establish roots and tillers before the crop's fate for the season is largely set. Two things matter most in that window:

1. Root Development

Humic and fulvic acids are known to support finer root hair development, which increases the surface area available for nutrient and water uptake — useful in both flooded and intermittently-irrigated paddy systems.

2. Tillering

Good tiller establishment is an important part of paddy production, but final yield depends on many factors including variety, planting density, nutrition, water management, pests and weather. Treat any product as one part of the overall crop programme.

Stage-by-Stage Application Guide

Here's a practical three-stage split that dealers in our network commonly recommend for paddy — adjust timing to your local transplanting calendar:

Stage 1 · Basal Dose

Before or During Transplanting

Apply as a basal dose into the puddled field a day or two before or immediately during transplanting. This gets the humic and fulvic acids working in the root zone from day one, supporting early root establishment.

Stage 2 · Active Growth

Tillering Stage (2–3 Weeks After Transplanting)

A second application during active tillering supports the plant through its most tiller-productive phase. This is typically when farmers notice the clearest visual difference versus untreated fields.

Stage 3 · Reproductive Phase

Panicle Initiation

A final application around panicle initiation supports grain filling and helps the plant carry through to a fuller harvest, particularly on soils prone to micronutrient deficiency.

Application Tips From the Field

  • Apply into standing water where possible so granules disperse evenly across the field.
  • Avoid mixing directly with high-salt fertilizers in the same handful — apply as separate passes.
  • Store packs in a dry place; granules are moisture-sensitive.
  • Keep field records of dosage and timing each season — this makes it easier to fine-tune next season's plan with your dealer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nirmala Agri Vita Green Gold made of? +

It is a granulated soil conditioner combining humic acid, fulvic acid, amino acids, seaweed extract and bentonite, formulated to support root development and nutrient uptake in vegetables, fruit crops and paddy.

What is the recommended dosage of Green Gold for paddy? +

The indicative dosage is around 10 to 12 kg per acre, though the exact amount can vary with soil type and crop stage. It is available in 1 kg and 10 kg packs. Always check the current product label before application.

When should Green Gold be applied during the paddy growing cycle? +

It is commonly applied at three key stages — as a basal dose before or during transplanting, again during the active tillering stage, and once more around panicle initiation — to support root growth, tiller number and grain filling respectively.

Can Green Gold be used with chemical fertilizer? +

Yes. Green Gold is typically used alongside a farmer's existing fertilizer programme rather than as a full replacement, helping improve nutrient efficiency and root development without requiring a complete change in practice.

Is Green Gold suitable for crops other than paddy? +

Yes, it is marketed for a range of vegetables and fruit crops in addition to paddy, given its role in supporting general root development and micronutrient uptake.
