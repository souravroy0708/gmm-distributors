Jackpot Dhamaka

Promotional Campaign

Organic Farming, Healthy Living

Jackpot Dhamaka is GMM Distributors' farmer-facing promotional campaign encouraging chemical-free, debt-free and self-reliant farming. The campaign is associated with our Jackpot range of soil boosters.

Promotional items featured as part of the campaign have included agricultural spray equipment, mobile phones, raincoats and umbrellas, as communicated from time to time.
