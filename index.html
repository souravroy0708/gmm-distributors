About GMM Distributors

Trusted Agricultural Products Supplier in Hooghly, West Bengal

GMM DISTRIBUTORS PRIVATE LIMITED is an India-based agricultural products company incorporated in 2020 and registered with the Registrar of Companies, Kolkata.

Based in Arambagh, Hooghly, West Bengal, the company operates in the chemicals and agricultural products sector, supplying organic manure, bio-fertilizers and related products, and is developing its distribution portfolio to serve diverse customer requirements across India.

Our approach is built around quality, responsible product use, customer service and long-term partnerships.
